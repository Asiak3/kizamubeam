# Code of Conduct

KizamuBeam is an open-source project built by people with different backgrounds, skills, and approaches to software development. We want it to be a welcoming place to use, discuss, test, and improve the software.

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone participating in KizamuBeam project spaces, including maintainers and contributors.

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment or discrimination, regardless of experience level, age, disability, neurodivergence, ethnicity, nationality, race, religion, gender identity or expression, sex, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics.

You do not have to agree with someone to treat them respectfully. Good-faith questions, technical disagreement, and constructive criticism are welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and helpful, especially with newcomers and people using unfamiliar tools or hardware.
- Discuss ideas, code, results, and risks rather than attacking the people behind them.
- Give actionable feedback when possible, and accept feedback without making it personal.
- Respect different development methods. **AI-assisted contributions are welcome**, provided contributors remain accountable for their work and follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). It is fine to question the quality, maintainability, licensing, or safety of AI-assisted code; personal attacks over tool choices are not.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and other people's time.
- Take physical safety seriously. If you identify a possible risk involving laser output, motion, homing, limits, or machine control, describe it clearly and avoid encouraging unsafe tests.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in project spaces:

- Harassment, intimidation, bullying, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Hate speech, slurs, discriminatory remarks, or attacks based on personal characteristics.
- Personal insults, deliberate humiliation, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexualized content directed at others.
- Threats of violence, encouragement of harm, or knowingly dangerous instructions presented as safe.
- Publishing or threatening to publish private information, such as personal contact details, credentials, or private communications, without permission.
- Retaliation against someone for raising a concern or reporting misconduct in good faith.

Disagreeing with a design decision, rejecting a pull request, pointing out defects, or discussing AI-generated code is not misconduct by itself. The manner in which we communicate matters.

## Scope

This policy applies to KizamuBeam's GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed community spaces. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in a public space or event.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this policy:

1. **For a routine disagreement**, you may ask participants to return to the technical topic or request maintainer moderation in the relevant thread, if you feel comfortable doing so.
2. **For harassment, threats, privacy concerns, or other sensitive reports**, do **not** post personal details or evidence publicly. Contact the project maintainer, [@negurvulkan](https://github.com/negurvulkan), using a private contact method they have made available. GitHub does not provide general-purpose private messaging, so please do not assume that mentioning an account is confidential.
3. **If you cannot find a private contact method**, you can open a brief issue asking for a *confidential Code of Conduct reporting channel*, without including the sensitive details. For abuse that violates GitHub's own policies, you may also use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies).

When reporting, share only what is necessary: what happened, where and when it occurred, and links or screenshots if appropriate. Please avoid sharing other people's private information unnecessarily.

**Maintainer note:** A dedicated private reporting address or form should be added here when one is available. Until then, the procedure above is an interim route and should not be presented as guaranteed confidential communication.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports as fairly and promptly as practical, consider the context and severity, and take proportionate action. Depending on the situation, this may include requesting a change in behavior, issuing a warning, hiding or removing content, locking a discussion, declining contributions, restricting participation, or banning a participant from project spaces.

Reports will be handled discreetly to the extent practical, but absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Information may need to be shared with people responsible for moderation or with the hosting platform when necessary for safety or enforcement.

Maintainers are subject to the same standards. If a concern involves the maintainer and no independent project moderator is available, consider reporting the relevant conduct directly through GitHub's platform reporting tools.

We will not tolerate retaliation against people who make good-faith reports. Deliberately false reports made to harass someone may themselves be treated as misconduct.

## Questions and changes

This Code of Conduct is intended to support a healthy community, not to discourage honest feedback or technical debate. Suggestions for improving the policy are welcome through a normal GitHub issue or pull request, provided no sensitive case details are disclosed.

Thank you for helping make KizamuBeam a place where people can build useful things together.
